Full recommended tab structure + features per tab
1. Dashboard Overview (you have this)
Stat cards: CSR Capital Committed, Active Co-Funded Pilots, Testbeds Sponsored, MCA CSR-1 Compliance 
Add: Recent activity feed ("XYZ University submitted a new proposal", "Milestone 2 approved for Project ABC")
Add: Quick-action buttons (Browse Marketplace, View Pending Approvals)
Add: Domain-wise engagement chart (which sectors you're funding most)
2. University R&D Marketplace (you have the shell, empty state shown)

When populated, each project card needs:

Project title, university name, domain tag, short abstract
Stage indicator (Prototype / Needs Funding / Needs Mentor / Ready for Testbed)
Team size / faculty mentor name
"Express Interest" / "Commit CSR Funding" / "Offer Mentorship" action buttons
Attached documents/proposal PDF, images of prototype
Funding ask amount (if specified)
Filter bar (you have domain filters — also add: stage, university, funding range)
Sort options (newest, most funded, closing soon)
3. Active Co-Funded Projects
List/table of projects this company is currently funding or mentoring
Per project: milestone tracker (visual progress bar), next deliverable due date
Fund disbursement history (tranche 1 released, tranche 2 pending milestone approval)
Communication thread with university team (or link to messaging)
Document repository (proposals, reports, test results)
Status: On Track / Delayed / Completed / At Risk
4. CSR Compliance & Ledger
This is important — likely your most legally-sensitive tab
CSR spend ledger: date, project, amount, category (as per Schedule VII of Companies Act)
Auto-generated CSR-1 / CSR-2 report exports (PDF/Excel) for MCA filing
Fund utilization certificates upload/download
Audit trail (immutable log of all fund movements)
Annual CSR budget vs. committed vs. utilized (visual breakdown)
5. Field Testbed Deployments
Map view of Jharkhand showing deployment locations (district-wise pins)
Per deployment: which project, which district/block, deployment date, status (Planned/Live/Completed)
Field data/feedback if solution is live (usage stats, beneficiary count)
Photos/evidence of on-ground implementation
Tabs you're likely missing
6. Notifications / Alerts
New matching projects in your domain of interest
Milestone approvals needed from you
Compliance deadline reminders
7. Company Profile / Settings
Company details, GSTIN, CIN, sector expertise
Team members (multiple people from same company can have accounts — need role management: who can approve funding vs. just view)
Preferences (which domains to auto-notify on)
8. Reports & Analytics (could merge into Dashboard or be separate)
Impact reports: patents generated, startups spawned, jobs created from funded projects
Downloadable annual impact summary for company's own CSR reporting/PR use
